Privacy Policy

Japan Travel Guide

Effective date: 18 September 2026

Contact: zeuslab58100@gmail.com

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application currently named Japan Travel Guide ("the App") handles information and device permissions. The App is designed as a travel companion for visitors to Japan and includes translation, voice features, Japanese OCR, location-based discovery, transport tools, currency conversion, local travel databases, favorites and itinerary planning.

The App does not require a user account and does not operate a proprietary developer backend for its core features.

2. Location

The App may request access to your device location only when you use features that need it, such as nearby places, "Near Me", current-origin transport functions or other GPS-based travel tools.

  • Location is requested contextually, when the relevant feature is used.
  • The App does not request background location access.
  • The App does not continuously track your location.
  • The developer does not store your location on a proprietary server.
  • You may deny or revoke location permission at any time in Android settings.
  • On supported Android versions, you may be able to grant approximate rather than precise location.

3. Camera, Photos and Japanese OCR

Camera access may be requested when you choose photo translation, Japanese text recognition or live camera translation. Access to images or the photo library may be requested when you choose an existing image for translation.

OCR processing uses Google ML Kit components on the device. The App does not upload your camera images or selected photos to a developer-owned server.

4. Microphone, Speech Recognition and Text-to-Speech

Microphone access is requested only when you activate voice input or dictation. The App itself does not store microphone recordings on a developer-owned server.

Speech recognition and text-to-speech are provided through your device operating system and/or the speech services configured on your device. Depending on your device, language pack and service provider, voice processing may occur locally or may be processed by that provider under its own privacy terms.

5. Translation and Google ML Kit

The App uses Google ML Kit for supported translation and Japanese text-recognition features. Translation models may be downloaded to your device. Core ML Kit processing used by the App is designed to operate on-device once the required models are available.

Google services and downloaded models are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. Local Databases and Favorites

The App includes local travel databases containing information about attractions, transport, food and shopping locations. These datasets are stored within the application/device.

Favorites and similar user preferences are stored locally on your device using SQLite databases. They are not synchronized to a developer-owned cloud account.

7. External Services and Internet Connections

Some features require internet access or open third-party services. These may include:

  • Google Maps for map searches and transit/directions links.
  • Wikipedia for place descriptions and related information.
  • Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons for open-data place information and images.
  • Frankfurter / European Central Bank data for currency exchange-rate information.
  • External websites associated with places when you choose to open them.

When you use an external service, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, device/browser information, requested URL and other data necessary to deliver the service. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies.

8. OpenStreetMap Data

Some offline place and travel datasets distributed with the App are derived from OpenStreetMap data. The App uses these datasets locally for search, discovery, transport and nearby-place features. OpenStreetMap data is provided under the Open Database License (ODbL).

More information is available at openstreetmap.org/copyright.

9. Personal Data Collected by the Developer

The App does not require registration and, in its current configuration, the developer does not operate a proprietary account system or analytics backend that collects your name, email address, travel history, location history, photos, voice recordings or favorites.

If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the contents of your message will be processed solely for the purpose of responding to your request and maintaining necessary correspondence.

10. Data Sharing and Sale

The developer does not sell personal data. The App does not intentionally share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be transmitted to third-party services only when necessary for a feature you choose to use, as described above.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local favorites and preferences remain on your device until you remove them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Because the App does not maintain a proprietary cloud user account, there is no remote user profile to delete.

Email correspondence sent to the developer may be retained as reasonably necessary to answer support requests, document communications and comply with applicable legal obligations.

12. Permissions and Your Choices

You remain in control of Android permissions. You may grant, deny or later revoke permissions such as location, camera, microphone and photo/media access through your device settings. Some App features may not function if the required permission is denied.

13. Children

The App is a general travel utility and is not specifically directed to children. The App does not knowingly operate an account system designed to collect personal information from children.

14. Security

The App is designed to minimize collection of personal information and to keep core travel data and user favorites locally on the device. No method of electronic processing or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's features, data practices, third-party services or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
